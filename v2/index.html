Hi, I'm Cameron. I'm drawn to one question asked a few different ways: how does a machine find its way? Sometimes the machine is a drone or an aircraft. Sometimes it's a model trying to make sense of biology.

This past summer I was at Stanford's Autonomous Systems Lab, teaching a vision-language navigation model built for quadcopters to fly a fixed-wing aircraft. The summer before, I worked on self-driving at Wayve.
