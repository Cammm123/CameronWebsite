Research

vision-language navigation
2026
Stanford Autonomous Systems Laboratory
Research Intern — Summer 2026

Worked within a team of two postdocs and three PhD researchers on WORLDS, a vision-language navigation harness originally built for quadcopter drones. My work extended it to a new class of aircraft and built out the evaluation infrastructure around it.

  • Extended the WORLDS navigation harness from quadcopters to a fixed-wing C-12 aircraft, adapting it to new flight dynamics and sensor geometry
  • Built CalCity, a benchmark for evaluating navigation-language models against real Californian urban environments
  • Designed a pilot-facing route-planning interface on top of the harness
  • Ran systematic failure analysis on agent navigation decisions to identify where language grounding breaks down

Alongside this, I've been reading through the broader vision-language navigation literature — WORLDS, HTNav, UrbanNav, OpenUAV, and AerialVLA — using WORLDS as a baseline for comparison.
